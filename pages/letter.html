SOMETHING FROM THE HEART

To you,
with all my heart.

♡

My girl, ♡

I don't even know where to begin, because there's so much I want to tell you, and sometimes it feels like words could never fully explain everything I feel.

Ever since you came into my life, I've found myself thinking about you constantly. I keep checking my phone, hoping to see a message from you, and I can't stop smiling whenever we talk. My eyes light up in ways I don't even notice until someone could probably see it for themselves. You have become such a beautiful part of my days, and I don't think you realize just how much happiness you bring me.

But it's not only about the smiles or the butterflies. It's about something much deeper that you've awakened in me.

You've made me want to open my heart again. To trust again. To stop holding back the feelings I have so much to give. I want to let myself love openly, honestly, and wholeheartedly. I want to show you just how much love I can give, not because I have to prove anything to you, but because loving you is something I genuinely want to do.

There's so much about you that makes you special to me. The way you express your feelings so openly, the way you try to understand me, the way you want to know every little part of who I am. The way you take my worries seriously and try to help me through them. And the way you make it clear that you're serious about us. All of these little things mean more to me than I can explain. They make me feel like what we're building matters to you, too.

And when I think about what I want with you, I realize that I don't just want beautiful moments or sweet words. I want something real. I want a love where we choose each other not because we have to, but because we genuinely want to. A love where we can be completely ourselves, where neither of us has to hide the parts of who we are, and where we can talk about anything, even the difficult things.

I want us to feel safe enough to share our fears, our doubts, our dreams, and everything in between. I want us to learn how to understand each other even when we don't immediately see things the same way. I want to celebrate your happiness, support you when things get difficult, and keep discovering new things about you as time goes by.

I want you to know the real me, not just the easy or pretty parts, but the thoughts, feelings, dreams, and little pieces of my heart that make me who I am. I want to feel that I can be entirely myself with you and still be loved for exactly who I am. And I want to give you that same feeling, to love you as you are and to keep learning how to love you better.

And if I'm being completely honest with you, I want a future with you. I want to look back one day and remember how all of this began. I want us to have our own memories, our own little traditions, our own stories. I want to discover the world with you, grow alongside you, and face whatever life brings us together. I know we can't predict everything that lies ahead, but I know that I want to give this a real chance.

There's something about what I'm feeling that feels different to me. I feel so sure that I want to give this a chance, that I want to be present, to be honest with you, and to give this connection the care it deserves. I don't want to hide how much you mean to me or pretend that my feelings are smaller than they are.

I want to choose you, again and again, through the ordinary days and the extraordinary ones. I want to be someone you can trust, someone you can talk to, someone who listens and makes an effort to understand you. I want us to build something that belongs to both of us, something full of love, tenderness, honesty, and mutual effort.

And more than anything, I want to see you happy. Not because your happiness is my responsibility alone, but because you matter to me, and your happiness genuinely matters to me, too.

I don't know exactly what our future will look like, but I know what I hope for. I hope that we keep opening our hearts to each other. I hope that we keep choosing to understand each other. And I hope that, with time, we can build the kind of love that both of us can feel proud of and safe in.

I think that's what I want you to understand most: I'm not just excited about you. I want to know you, to love you, to grow with you, and to see where we can go together. I want to give us a chance to become something truly special.

And if there's one thing I don't want you to doubt, it's that my feelings for you are sincere. I'm here because I want to be here. I'm opening my heart because I want you to see what's inside it.

There's so much more I could say, but perhaps some things are better shown through the way we treat each other, one day at a time.

♡

I'm so glad you came into my life. And I'm so excited about the possibility of what we could become.
